Most geo applications need two halves: a front end that makes large data easy to explore, and a backend that can actually process it. [OnlyMap JS](/onlymap/overview) is the first, NIKA GeoEngine is the second, and they are built to meet in the middle. For more live OnlyMap maps, browse the examples on <a href="https://onlymap.nikaplanet.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">onlymap.nikaplanet.com ↗</a>.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Front end: OnlyMap JS" icon="map">Projection-aware WebGL maps from declarative HTML. Streams COG and Zarr rasters straight from storage, and polls live data.</Card>
  <Card title="Backend: NIKA GeoEngine" icon="server">Batch jobs that fan out on CPU, high-memory and GPU pools, behind role-based access, writing COG, Zarr and GeoJSON.</Card>

## How they connect

- **No tile server in between.** Workers write Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFF or Zarr; OnlyMap's `COGLayer` and `ZarrLayer` read only the parts on screen, by HTTP range request. See <a href="https://onlymap.nikaplanet.com/display-a-geotiff/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">a COG</a> and <a href="https://onlymap.nikaplanet.com/slice-a-zarr-cube/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">a Zarr cube</a> live.
- **Progress is just data.** A layer with `refresh="2s"` polls the job's task list, so tiles light up on the map as tasks finish. <a href="https://onlymap.nikaplanet.com/san-francisco-last-mile/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">See polling live ↗</a>.
- **Credentials stay out of markup.** `OmMap.configureData()` adds your token to every data-layer request, polling included, so the task list can sit behind your app's authentication.
- **Rasters are fetched directly by the browser.** `COGLayer` and `ZarrLayer` do not route through `configureData`. Serve COG results same-origin through your app, where the browser sends the user's session cookie, or from a CORS-enabled store. Authenticated Zarr stores are not supported yet, so use Zarr for results that may be public and COG behind your app for sensitive ones.
- **Access control carries through.** A user who isn't cleared for a restricted worker is refused at the gateway, and the map says so.

## The front-end code

The whole map is markup. Your app API proxies two calls to NIKA GeoEngine: create the job, and return its tasks as GeoJSON.

```html index.html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://unpkg.com/@nika-js/onlymap@0.12.1/dist/onlymapjs.css">
<script type="module">
  import { OmMap } from "https://unpkg.com/@nika-js/onlymap@0.12.1";
  OmMap.configureData({ headers: { Authorization: `Bearer ${sessionToken}` } });
</script>

<om-map basemap="satellite" center="[5.56, 52.52]" zoom="10">
  <!-- results: a COG written by the batch job, served same-origin by your app and streamed by range request -->
  <om-layer id="ndvi" type="COGLayer"
            src="/results/2026-11/flevoland/ndvi.tif"
            min="0" max="1" colormap="viridis"></om-layer>

  <!-- progress: one polygon per task, re-polled every 2 seconds -->
  <om-layer id="tasks" type="GeoJsonLayer"
            data="https://app.example.com/api/jobs/j-7q2c9xk4/tasks.geojson" refresh="2s"
            get-fill-color="$fill" get-line-color="[255,255,255,140]"
            line-width-min-pixels="1" pickable></om-layer>

  <om-widget type="legend" position="bottom-end"></om-widget>
</om-map>
```

## What teams build with the pair

  <Card title="Flood extent within hours" icon="water">
    **Need:** where the water is after a storm, while clouds still cover optical satellites.

    **Backend:** Argo Events starts a batch job when a Sentinel-1 radar scene lands; a CPU worker compares it with a dry baseline, tile by tile.

    **On the map:** the flood mask over the satellite basemap, with affected roads and facilities as `PopupLayer` badges. Try <a href="https://onlymap.nikaplanet.com/label-many-features/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">labelling many features</a>.

    **Need:** flag construction across very high-resolution imagery of sites few people may see.

    **On the map:** before and after imagery as COGs served through your app, a layer switcher, and detected footprints as polygons for review.

## Why the pair works

| Need | OnlyMap JS | NIKA GeoEngine |
|---|---|---|
| Huge rasters | Streams only what is on screen | Processes them on machines sized to fit |
| Many scenes | Shows progress task by task | Fans out across pods |
| Sensitive data | Keeps tokens out of markup; reads sensitive COGs through your app | Restricts workers to cleared roles; data stays in your cluster |
| Projections | Draws in national grids and equal-area projections | Reprojects with GDAL and PROJ in every worker |
